Terms and Privacy Policy

1. What {{ app_name }} does

{{ app_name }} helps students plan a food and essentials budget, compare shelf prices at nearby Durban stores and keep a shopping list. It does not sell goods and does not pay for anything on your behalf. Prices come from third-party sources, can be out of date and are shown as a guide only.

2. Information we collect

Your name, email address, cellphone number, residential address (and the map position we work out from it), gender, race, date of birth and the last four digits of your South African ID number. If you sign in with Microsoft we receive your name and email address from Microsoft. We also store the budgets, lists and preferences you create.

We check your ID number to make sure it is valid and to stop the same person registering twice, then keep only a one-way fingerprint of it plus the last four digits. We cannot recover the full number.

3. Why we use it

To run your account, sort stores by distance from where you live, and show relevant prices. Gender, race and date of birth are optional context for reporting and are not used to decide what you see.

4. Who else sees it

Addresses you type or pin are sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to find their position on the map. Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap. Store and price lookups send your approximate area (not your name or email) to the price data provider.

5. Your rights

You can update your details on the Profile page. You may ask for your account and its data to be deleted; deleting an account removes your budgets, lists and preferences.

6. Acceptable use

Do not use another person's identity number or details, and do not try to disrupt the service.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

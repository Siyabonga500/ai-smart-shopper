About us

Helping Durban students stretch every rand.

{{ app_name }} started with a simple question from students living on the NSFAS meal allowance: where is it cheapest, and is it worth the trip? We built one place to plan the month, compare the shops and keep track of what was spent.

What you can do

  • Set a monthly budget of up to {{ nsfas_allowance|zar }}, split by category or as one combined amount.
  • Compare a product’s price at every nearby store and see how far each store is from you.
  • Build a shopping list that warns you before you go over budget, with cheaper alternatives.
  • See the stores on a map in the best order to visit them, and tick items off as you buy them.
  • Look back at every trip: what you spent, where, and how much you saved.
